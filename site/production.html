Tier assumptions and sources

The docs specify 10% faster cycles and 10% higher output per production tier. They do not specify the baseline, stacking, or integer rounding. Change these assumptions to match your in-game results.

Workbench and vehicle output need additional recipes and craft times; they are not included. This estimates processing capacity, not unlimited manual mining.
