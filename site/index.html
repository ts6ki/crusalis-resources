What the numbers mean

Each count is the number of owned territories carrying that resource, not production or income. One territory can carry more than one resource. Ownership follows town claims, not wartime occupation.

Population

Registered residents across a nation’s towns, including offline players. Search and sorting don’t change it.

Sides

Allies and Axis follow the confirmed nation list. Bohemia-Moravia is Axis; Nationalist China, Communist China and Shaanxi are Allies. Nations not on the list are under Other.

Factories and gaps

Factories without a known type or tier are listed as “Unspecified type.” N/A means the source doesn’t report that resource.

Updates

A new snapshot is published every 2 hours, though schedules can run late. Reload fetches the latest published snapshot; it doesn’t start a new scan. Data over three hours old is marked as possibly stale.
